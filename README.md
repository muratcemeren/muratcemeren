### 👨‍💻 About Me
Hello! I am Murat Cem. I have been in the software world since 2016 and have been developing projects as a Full-Stack Developer for about 10 years. During this long period, I have had the chance to experience firsthand how technology has evolved and participated in many different projects.

For me, software development means solving complex problems with user-friendly and clean code structures. I especially enjoy developing interfaces that prioritize user experience with modern web technologies like React and Vue, and building solid backend architectures with PHP and JavaScript.

With my ten years of experience, I have a perspective that focuses not just on writing code, but on the sustainability and performance of the project. I make it a habit to stay up-to-date by following new libraries and methodologies in this constantly evolving industry.
